# SR-001 — Mask PII in extracted fields before persistence

## Control Statement

The extraction pipeline MUST detect and mask PII fields (full names,
government IDs, addresses, employment details) before any extracted
record is persisted to the fields data store. Unmasked PII MUST NOT
appear in logs, traces, or analytics events.

## Cited Obligation

- Threat **T-03** (Unmasked PII Leak in Extracted Fields).
- Australian Privacy Act 1988 — Australian Privacy Principle 11
  (Security of Personal Information).
- Client internal standard **MC-DH-04** §4.2.

## Measurement Method

- Daily DLP (Data Loss Prevention) scan over the extracted-fields
  store using the client's standard PII detector library.
- Any hit (count > 0) opens a Sev-1 incident and pauses the
  extraction pipeline until cleared.

## Why This SR

NFR-002 commits to an accuracy floor for classification, which means
the pipeline will reliably surface documents containing PII —
making PII handling a load-bearing concern from day one, not a
post-launch retrofit.
