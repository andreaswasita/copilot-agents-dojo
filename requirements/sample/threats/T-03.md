# T-03 — Unmasked PII Leak in Extracted Fields

**Threat actor:** A downstream analyst (insider) or an external
attacker with read access to the extracted-fields data store.

**Target asset:** Personal Identifiable Information present in
extracted document fields (full names, government IDs, addresses,
employment details).

**Damaging outcome:** Re-identification of individuals named in
safety-incident and contract documents, in breach of the Australian
Privacy Act (APP 11) and the client's internal data-handling
standard MC-DH-04.

**Modelling reference:** STRIDE → Information Disclosure. Recorded
in `threat-model-2026-05-18.md`.
