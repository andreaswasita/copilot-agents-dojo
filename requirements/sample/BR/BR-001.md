# BR-001 — Reduce bilingual document-processing cost

## Context

Regulated mining client processes ~120k bilingual (English / Simplified
Chinese) documents per quarter through a manual classification and
extraction workflow. Cost per document is ~AUD 7.20; cycle time is
> 36 hours per document.

## Business Outcome

Reduce per-document processing cost by 30% within four quarters of
go-live, without increasing regulatory exposure.

## Measurement

- Cost per document, measured monthly from the finance ledger tagged
  to the workflow cost centre.
- Compared against the FY25 baseline of AUD 7.20 / doc.

## Ratification

Architecture Review Board, 2026-05-20. Decision record: `arb-2026-05-20`.
