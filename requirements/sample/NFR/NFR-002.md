# NFR-002 — Classification accuracy floor

## Quality Attribute

Functional accuracy.

## Operating Envelope

- Gold set: 2000 documents, balanced across the five categories and
  EN/ZH languages, refreshed quarterly by the data steward.
- Production traffic: rolling 7-day window, sampled at 5%.

## Measurement Method

- Offline: nightly evaluation against the gold set; results published
  to the model evaluation dashboard.
- Online: human reviewers audit a 5% sample of routed documents
  weekly; precision computed against reviewer adjudication.

## Why This NFR

Without an accuracy floor, the cost saving from FR-001 evaporates into
downstream rework. The 99.5% / 95% split separates *internal*
performance (gold) from *real-world* performance (production sample).
