# NFR-001 — Classification latency under load

## Quality Attribute

Latency.

## Operating Envelope

- 50 documents per minute sustained, 90-minute peak windows during
  end-of-shift uploads.
- 60 / 40 EN / ZH mix, weighted by historical ingest profile.
- Document size: 95th percentile = 6 MB, 99th percentile = 18 MB.

## Measurement Method

Synthetic probe runs every 60s from the operations VPC, posting a
fixed corpus of 100 representative documents and measuring end-to-end
classification latency. Results captured in Prometheus; SLO burn rate
alerts wired to PagerDuty.

## Why This NFR

BR-001 targets 30% cost reduction. The cost lever depends on cycle
time, and cycle time depends on classification latency at the head of
the pipeline. An NFR slower than this target re-introduces the manual
triage step the BR is trying to remove.
